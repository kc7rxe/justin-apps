# Justin's Android Apps

Permanent download hosting for Justin Willits' Android apps. Each release holds one APK. Tap a release asset on your tablet to download and install.

## Apps

- **Sound to Sheet Music** — hum or sing a melody and turn it into sheet music
- **Schematic Generator** — draw and export electronic circuit schematics
- **PCB Maker** — lay out printed circuit boards
- **Plasma CAD CAM** — design parts and generate CNC plasma cutting paths
- **Rotary Groove CAM** — unwrap a cylinder, draw grooves/features, and export CAM for rotary cutting
